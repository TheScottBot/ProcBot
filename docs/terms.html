Terms of Service

Last updated 18 October 2026

The short version: it's a free history bot. Use it, enjoy it, don't be a dick with it.

What it is

ProcBot posts real royal proclamations of England, Ireland and Scotland (1485-1714), or summaries of them, for history and fun. Some are the voice of a state that persecuted Catholics, dissenters, Jews, Romani people, the Irish and others; posting them isn't endorsing them. /wanted and /decree are jokes: /decree's proclamations are made up, and marked as not real. Nothing it posts is legal advice, and none of it is law any more.

Don't be a dick

  • Don't use it to harass, threaten or target anyone. /wanted is a joke between friends, not a way to go after someone.
  • Don't use the proclamations' bigotry against real people.
  • Don't pass off a /decree as a real proclamation.
  • Don't try to break, overload or abuse the bot.
  • Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, which apply to everything you do with it.

Server admins

You choose what the bot shows in your server with /setup, and the Proclamation of the Day follows its channel's filters. The content filters work by keyword, so they're good but not perfect. If something gets through that shouldn't, hide it with /setup hide, and please report it so the filter can be fixed. What's appropriate for your server is your call, and your responsibility.

The texts

The full texts are the Text Creation Partnership's transcriptions of Early English Books Online, which are in the public domain (CC0). The summaries are Robert Steele's, from his 1910 calendar, read from a scan, so with the odd misprint. Each proclamation links to its source.

No guarantees

The bot is provided as-is, for free, with no warranty. It may be down, wrong, changed or shut off at any time. It can also be removed from any server that uses it to break these terms.
